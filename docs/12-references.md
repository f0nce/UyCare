## Review limits

Product claims come from public official descriptions, not independent feature testing. The literature synthesis uses accessible abstracts, publisher text and author versions. Detailed replication or a systematic review protocol is outside this pre-defense package. Where a page did not establish a product feature, the comparison records Not established.
