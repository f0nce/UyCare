# Existing system comparison

## Method and relevance

Three systems are compared against ten consistent criteria. Profi.kz is a local-market comparator with direct overlap in specialist selection. Airtasker is an international request-and-offer comparator. Taskrabbit provides a contrasting provider-booking model. The comparison uses the official pages [S11–S13], accessed on 21 October 2026, rather than anonymous reviews or assumptions about hidden features.

Yes means the reviewed page explicitly describes the capability. Not established means that this review did not verify it; it never means the product lacks it. UyCare entries are proposed requirements, not implemented capabilities. Country availability, commercial terms and feature details require verification before making a market-entry claim.

## Implications

The basic request-and-offer pattern is not novel. UyCare must demonstrate the quality of its implementation and the usefulness of its residential workflow, rather than claiming to be the first marketplace. Organisation-owned requests, recorded selection and a category suggestion are candidate differentiators; the reviewed sources do not prove that competitors lack them.

Profi.kz is the most relevant local comparator, so the next validation step is a scenario walkthrough with potential users. Taskrabbit helps test whether customers prefer direct booking. Airtasker provides a useful comparison for offer selection and payment coordination. UyCare's initial direct-payment scope reduces implementation effort but provides no escrow or platform payment protection.

This is a documented feature review, not a hands-on product audit. No composite winner score is calculated: the criteria have not been weighted by actual users. Screenshots or dated field observations can supplement the source register after authorized walkthroughs.
