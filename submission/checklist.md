# First pre-defense submission checklist

Submission date: 24 October, assumed 2026. Actual submission commit and tag: not yet selected. Repository: https://github.com/f0nce/UyCare. YouTrack: not created.

## Prepared artifacts

- Project README, proposal, scope and team allocation.
- Focused literature review with eight published scientific sources, two additional research reports and official supporting sources.
- Three-system comparison using ten criteria and four technical decisions with three alternatives each.
- Eighteen functional and twelve nonfunctional requirements with acceptance criteria and traceability.
- Editable context, use-case, component and lifecycle diagrams with SVG exports.
- Portable browser prototype and demonstration instructions.
- Verification, user-baseline and ML evaluation plans.
- Twenty-eight prioritised planning tasks with owners, proposed deadlines and acceptance criteria.
- Supervisor feedback and interview instruments.

## Obligations that remain external or unverified

- Conduct 3–5 real user discussions or obtain a substantive client agreement.
- Obtain substantive supervisor feedback and record resulting revisions.
- Create YouTrack, map actual accounts, import tasks and add its live link.
- Review the published preparation branch with another team member and address their comments.
- Demonstrate genuine progressive contributions from each student. A generated starting package does not meet this historical requirement.
- Confirm the academic calendar and language scope for ML.

## Submission procedure

After peer review, merge the intended submission into main. Record its commit hash, completed work, limitations and outstanding tasks in submission/release-notes.md. Create the annotated pre-defense-1 tag on that exact commit and push it once. Do not move it later. Grant the supervisor and committee access before the presentation. Repeat with pre-defense-2 and final-pre-defense at the relevant stages.

## Demonstration sequence

Show README and structure, then source history. Trace one actual YouTrack task to its branch, PR, reviewer comment, revision and artifact. Open an editable diagram and its export. Run the prototype and explain its limitations. Show the verification plan and open evidence items. Each member explains their real contribution. Do not present planned backend/mobile work as running software.
